Research

Paper Title: "When the Teacher Is the Worst Student: Source Selection Failure in Expert-Free Online Transfer Learning Beyond Its Validated Regime"

A reproducibility study of the Expert-Free Online Transfer Learning (EF-OnTL) algorithm, extended to a new and more complex environment.

Algorithm
Expert-Free Online Transfer Learning (EF-OnTL)
Baseline
A 2025 doctoral thesis evaluating EF-OnTL across several environments, you can find the doctoral thesis here: Expert-Free Online Transfer Learning
New environment
Pac-Man, simulated as a multi-agent setting with five concurrent agents
Status
In progress, close to completion

Background

The baseline thesis evaluates EF-OnTL under a teacher-student framework. Neither Cart-Pole nor Pac-Man is natively multi-agent, so a multi-agent setting is simulated by running several parallel instances that exchange knowledge during training. In the original work this was done with five parallel instances of Cart-Pole.

My contribution

  1. Reimplementing the original work to test whether the result can still be reproduced on commodity hardware.
  2. Extending the evaluation to Pac-Man, a non-native multi-agent environment that is substantially more complex than Cart-Pole.

Research questions

  • If the configuration used by the author is transferred to a more complex, non-native multi-agent system, are there any obvious changes?
  • Is commodity hardware sufficient, or does the method assume a research cluster?
