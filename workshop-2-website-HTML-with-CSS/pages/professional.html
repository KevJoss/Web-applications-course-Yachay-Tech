IN PROGRESS 2026

When the teacher is the worst student: source selection failure in expert-free online transfer learning beyond its validated regime

A reproducibility study of the Expert-Free Online Transfer Learning (EF-OnTL) algorithm, extended to a new and more complex environment.

ALGORITHM Expert-Free Online Transfer Learning (EF-OnTL)
BASELINE 2025 doctoral thesis across several environments - arXiv:2501.15495
ENVIRONMENT Pac-Man, simulated multi-agent with five concurrent agents
STATUS Submitted to the WAIAMS 2026 for peer review
MY CONTRIBUTION
  1. Reimplement the original work to test whether the result reproduces on commodity hardware.
  2. Extend the evaluation to Pac-Man, a non-native multi-agent environment far more complex than Cart-Pole.
RESEARCH QUESTIONS
  • Does the author's configuration hold when moved to a more complex, non-native multi-agent system?
  • Is commodity hardware enough, or does the method assume a research cluster?

Background: the baseline evaluates EF-OnTL under a teacher–student framework. Neither Cart-Pole nor Pac-Man is natively multi-agent, so the setting is simulated with several parallel instances exchanging knowledge during training — five instances of Cart-Pole in the original work.
