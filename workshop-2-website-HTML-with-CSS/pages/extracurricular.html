CompuFest 2026

A large open-house event where students present the projects they built during the semester. The audience includes the university community, companies, and Yachay Tech graduates now working at leading tech firms.

You can find more information about the event in this website: CompuFest 2026.

Inter-university Hackathon

Currently in organisation. Three companies each bring a real problem from their own operations, and student teams have three days to build a usable solution. The companies are not necessarily from the tech sector, which is part of the point: it forces us to apply what we know to unfamiliar domains.

What participants get out of it:

  1. Applying coursework knowledge to a problem with real constraints
  2. Direct contact with companies and their engineering teams
  3. Experience working under a deadline with a team
