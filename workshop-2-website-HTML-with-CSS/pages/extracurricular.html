Inter-university Hackathon

IN ORGANISATION

Three companies each bring a real problem from their own operations; student teams have three days to build a usable solution. The companies are not necessarily from the tech sector — that is the point: it forces us to apply what we know to unfamiliar domains.

WHAT PARTICIPANTS TAKE AWAY
  1. Coursework applied to a problem with real constraints
  2. Direct contact with companies and their engineering teams
  3. Working under a deadline, with a team
